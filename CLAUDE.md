# TD linedraw

A TouchDesigner port of LingDong-/linedraw, generated by `build_linedraw.py`.
linedraw has no vault hub of its own yet. It's listed under "nearby line-drawing work" in `~/Documents/Ro-Vault/01-Projects/td-soft-body.md`.
`README.md` documents the project and has a table of every file. Update that table when files are added, renamed or removed.

## Conventions
- The current version sits at the top level under a stable name. Superseded iterations go in `versions/`; don't edit them.
- `shaders/` are reference copies of the GLSL embedded in the builder. Change the builder first, then refresh the copy.
